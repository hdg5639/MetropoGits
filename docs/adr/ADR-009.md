# ADR-009: Polygon-aware district allocation and City Scene v0.2

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Decision owners: Project maintainer
- Applies to: City Scene geometry, hierarchical layout, validation, and rendering

## Context

`city/0.1` treats every district as a non-overlapping axis-aligned rectangle.
That invariant makes allocation and validation simple, but it also forces a
large child subtree to reserve every empty corner of its bounding box. On the
GJ-Cloud reference repository this repeatedly produces an L-shaped Ops area,
large holes inside VM, and unusable strips around Auth and Portal. ADR-008
stopped painting those empty cells, but the allocator still reserves them, so
other districts cannot use the recovered land.

The next layout must let sibling districts interlock without inventing source
entities or collapsing the directory hierarchy. A bounding rectangle alone
cannot distinguish a valid concave footprint from a real overlap, so this is a
public scene-contract change rather than a renderer-only adjustment.

## Decision

Introduce City Scene `city/0.2` and layout `hierarchical-paged/1.11`.

- Every district has a required `footprint`: a canonical, ordered union of
  non-overlapping axis-aligned rectangles. Together those rectangles are the
  district's orthogonal polygonal allocation area.
- `x`, `z`, `width`, and `depth` remain as the exact axis-aligned bounding box
  of the footprint. They are retained for camera fitting, indexing, and older
  geometry helpers; overlapping sibling bounding boxes are allowed.
- Sibling allocation is validated against footprint rectangles, not bounding
  boxes. Sibling footprints may touch but may not overlap.
- Every direct building must be covered by its district footprint. Every child
  footprint must be covered by its parent footprint. Canonical district
  footprints therefore preserve the directory hierarchy even when their
  bounding boxes interlock.
- The layout packs child subtrees as deterministic orthogonal shapes on a fixed
  grid. Placement minimizes the resulting longest side, then bounding area,
  aspect imbalance, and stable coordinate/id tie-breakers. No random or
  force-directed step is permitted.
- Sibling footprints may touch at an edge but never overlap; the renderer uses
  their shared boundary as a street seam. Empty structural districts receive a
  minimal cell instead of disappearing.
- Buildings, UCG entities, evidence, roles, and relationships are unchanged.
  Network routes are regenerated from the relocated evidenced endpoints.
- The viewer consumes the canonical footprint directly for `city/0.2` and
  retains the ADR-008 derived-territory fallback for `city/0.1` artifacts.

The `city/0.1` schema and validator behavior remain available for existing
artifacts. New layout output uses only `city/0.2`; there is no silent in-place
reinterpretation of an old document.

## Hint compatibility

`hierarchical-paged/1.11` accepts only `city/0.2` scenes produced by the same
layout family. A `city/0.1` scene is rejected as a placement hint with a
diagnostic because its rectangle-only collision contract cannot safely express
interlocking geometry.

Within `city/0.2`, unchanged subtrees retain their shape and preferred anchor
when it remains collision-free. If a changed sibling invalidates that anchor,
the deterministic scorer chooses the lowest-cost replacement and reports the
movement through the existing hint diagnostics.

## Validation rules

- Footprint rectangles must have finite coordinates and positive dimensions,
  use canonical order, and not overlap within one district.
- A district AABB must equal the union bounds of its footprint exactly.
- Direct buildings must be fully covered by the union, not merely intersect it.
- Each child footprint rectangle must be fully covered by the parent union.
- Sibling footprint rectangles must not overlap.
- Existing ID, ordering, evidence, network, and building-collision checks stay
  in force.

## Alternatives considered

### Keep rectangle allocation and improve only the renderer

Rejected because ADR-008 already demonstrates its limit: empty land can be
hidden but cannot be reused, leaving the overall composition sparse.

### Store a free-form polygon ring

Rejected for this version because polygon clipping, holes, winding rules, and
cross-platform triangulation add complexity that the orthogonal city does not
need. A rectangle union expresses the required concavity with exact arithmetic.

### Flatten major services into unrelated top-level islands

Rejected because it would make the screenshot denser by weakening the real
directory hierarchy. Parent coverage remains a required invariant.

### Apply force-directed or stochastic packing

Rejected because it would undermine deterministic output and small-change
stability.

## Consequences

- VM, Ops, Auth, Portal, and similar siblings can occupy one another's former
  empty bounding-box corners while remaining structurally separate.
- Consumers that understand only `city/0.1` must reject `city/0.2` rather than
  assuming rectangle non-overlap.
- Layout hints intentionally reset once at the migration boundary; subsequent
  `city/0.2` runs regain versioned stability.
- The validator and renderer become slightly more complex, but collision and
  land ownership remain exact, deterministic, and explainable.
