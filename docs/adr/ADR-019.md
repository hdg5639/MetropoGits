# ADR-019 Quantitative road hierarchy and trunk planning

- Status: Grades, avenue reuse/reservation, urban backbone and continuity cleanup implemented; road-first trunk geometry, curves and roundabouts pending
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Authority: maintainer request for measurable road grades, consolidation and realistic junctions

## Decision

Keep semantic relationship kind separate from physical road grade. A dependency
with many underlying edges remains DEPENDENCY_ROAD, not HTTP_HIGHWAY or measured
traffic. Grades describe static relationship demand, not throughput or capacity.
Count unique edge IDs, source files, destination files, all endpoint files, and
edges crossing the existing major structural districts. Evidence records are not
extra dependencies. Unassigned endpoints are unknown, not another service.

Use fixed, versioned thresholds rather than per-city percentiles. Full-city
membership determines a segment's grade; selecting one path or filtering files
must not shrink or promote the road. Inspector distinguishes full-road metrics
from the active paths and preserves access to exact underlying relationships.

Initial calibration on junction-merge-r1 after outputs: non-access segment unique
edge counts at p25/p50/p75/p90/p95 are GJ 4/11/31/65/94 and OJ 4/11/34/88/131.
These are segment-weighted distributions, not independent-road statistics or a
universal urban design standard. The initial `road-hierarchy/0.1` policy is:

| Grade | Required static demand | Target width |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Access | Building access spur, regardless of demand | 0.7 |
| Alley | Remaining low-demand segments | 0.9 |
| Street | At least 4 unique edges and 3 endpoint files | 1.5 |
| Avenue | At least 24 unique edges and 8 endpoint files | 2.5 |
| Interdistrict trunk demand | At least 64 unique edges, 16 files, and 16 cross-major-district edges | 3.5 |

Width is capped by the existing certified clearance. A narrow, busy corridor is
reported as clearance-limited; it is not widened through a building. Colors and
widths communicate grade while HTTP/messaging keep their semantic colors.
This first slice changes neither UCG/City contracts nor stored routes, IDs,
building positions or Synthetic trajectories. It does not deliver highway geometry.

## Staged road planning

1. Derive and expose demand metrics, deterministic grades and clearance limits.
2. Use those metrics to evaluate continuous shared collectors/trunks, not just
   delete low-use segments. Prefer long straight runs, fewer turns and limited
   access points. Keep the frozen detour/turn bounds and atomic rerouting of
   every affected relationship; report failures rather than silently relaxing them.
3. Experiment with reserving trunk space before parcel placement inside existing
   directory ownership and stable major anchors. Relationship aggregation →
   trunks and access points → reserved space → parcels → local door connections.
   This is an opt-in layout-version experiment, not permission to replace the
   canonical directory-first layout or discard stability. Record incremental
   placement regressions and obtain a decision before any contract break.
4. Add gentle bends and selective roundabouts only with full-width obstacle
   clearance and exact updated stored paths. Prefer roundabouts at suitable
   surface-street mergers; do not scatter circles along trunks. Synthetic and
   selection must follow the same geometry. Representation changes need a
   compatibility decision before implementation.

Aggressive consolidation does not mean indiscriminate joining. Split near actual
destinations, when grade/access requirements differ, or when merging would cause
excessive detours. Sharing a junction never creates an A-to-D relationship from
A-to-B and C-to-D. A high grade alone proves neither a straight alignment nor a
safe roundabout site.

## Bounded continuous avenue reuse

`python -m scripts.road_consolidation <build> --out <new-directory> --avenues`
compares junction-consolidated routing against a third avenue pass. This option
includes the earlier parallel and junction passes but starts from fresh complete
0.2 routing; all three share distance and bend limits frozen against that input.
Default build/Action and the earlier comparison modes remain unchanged.

After the first two passes, select existing same-kind straight runs of at least
four lattice steps (14 world units). Every unit edge must carry at least 24 unique
edges and 8 distinct endpoint files, reusing the avenue demand rule. Continue
straight through junctions; gaps and turns split runs. Freeze these spines for the
pass. This selects demand-bearing geometry, not a deployment/service claim or a
highway classification. The decision report records their paths. Final visible
grades are recomputed from final memberships; frozen routing preferences do not
freeze the displayed demand grade along an entire spine.

Try removing non-spine, same-kind parallel stretches within two lattice steps
(7 units), split at branches/ports. Prefer fewer affected unique edges, then
longer stretches and canonical geometry. Route affected dependencies on remaining
pavement with search costs 80 per spine step and 100 per other step. This is only a
search preference: the frozen actual-distance and +2-bend constraints still apply.
Retain independent cost/distance/bend Pareto labels so a cheap long prefix cannot
discard a shorter feasible route. Access spurs remain excluded from through roads.

Commit replacements only if every affected route is valid, total traversal of
the frozen spines increases, and no protected spine loses its last actual user.
Otherwise roll back every replacement. Remove orphaned roads and rebuild exact
memberships/junctions; never add pavement. Result metadata `avenueConsolidation`
uses `avenue-consolidation/0.1` inside derived `visual-access/0.3`; UCG/City remain
unchanged. Report accepted/rejected candidates, reuse before/after and resource
limits. Search failures group unavailable/over-distance/over-bend outcomes;
they are not proof that no better global street plan exists.

Additional limits: 600 candidate attempts, 2M popped states, 100k labels/search.
The previous 100k atomic-edge/1M route-step input limits remain. Old 0.3 results
cannot be fed back to compound allowances. This pass may add turns on individual
merge/exit routes (within original limits), and freezes many useful streets rather
than solving a globally minimal avenue network. Sparse inputs with no qualifying
run safely keep their previous geometry.

## Acceptance

### Whole-network backbone trial

The maintainer's request to merge, divert and remove roads expands candidate
selection beyond neighboring parallel lines. In a separate opt-in comparison,
collapse each same-kind network into corridors between branch/entrance nodes,
including bends. Rank corridors by unique evidenced edge demand, fewer bends,
then shorter length and canonical geometry. Short ties preserve necessary local
connectors instead of preferring a long detour around their block. A maximum-demand spanning forest identifies
the initial shared backbone; it is a physical planning preference, not a new
semantic relationship or a claim that a tree alone can serve every dependency.

Try removing the non-backbone cycle corridors, rerouting all affected relations
onto remaining roads with a backbone preference. Keep rejected corridors when
connectivity, original distance or +2-bend bounds need them. Preserve endpoints,
buildings and source evidence; remove orphan pavement, not logical edges. This
does not require adjacency to a parallel road, a low road grade, or preservation
of every old avenue. Still start from fresh 0.2 and freeze all bounds before any
pass; default modes remain unchanged. Report full-city pavement, junctions,
detours, refusals and search caps, not just successful individual edits.

### Approved urban-mode detour policy (2026-10-18)

The maintainer explicitly approved a separate urban mode with up to 50% detour
and a short-route block allowance. `--urban` enables the whole-network backbone
pass after the existing three conservative passes; none of the default policies
change. In that fourth pass only, freeze each route's bounds against fresh 0.2:

- Let N be its original through-road lattice steps (exclude fixed access spurs).
  Maximum steps = max(floor(3*N/2), N+4). Four lattice steps are 14 world units:
  an out-and-back around one 7-unit block, not four additional blocks.
- At most four additional bends relative to the original complete route,
  including its unchanged entrance/exit headings.
- Never compound the allowance over passes or feed a 0.3 output back as input.
  Keep source/target, kind, evidence, fixed ports, buildings and access unchanged.

This is a bounded physical detour, not inferred runtime traffic. Remove a corridor
only after every affected dependency has a complete permitted replacement on
existing same-kind pavement; rollback the entire candidate on failure. Frozen
maximum-demand forest roads guide routing but do not invent reachability. Keep
bridges and necessary cross-links. The 2M-state/100k-label/2400-candidate caps
remain; report truncation as a limitation, not a globally optimal result.
Derived `backboneConsolidation.policy` identifies `urban-road/0.1`, distinct from
the conservative backbone trial; public UCG/City schemas and IDs do not change.
Record actual route-distance distribution and exceptions for short paths, not
only pavement reduction. Inspector and Synthetic use the final complete routes.
Curves and roundabouts are not delivered by this orthogonal routing step.

### Continuous-road cleanup after urban consolidation

`--continuity` is an opt-in comparison against `--urban`, not another relaxation
of its approved bounds. After pass four, first align complete routes on existing
same-kind pavement: no longer and no more bends than their current path. A cost
of 100 per lattice step plus 200 per heading change favors continuous alignments
(including entrance/exit turns); accept only an actual length/bend improvement.
Remove orphan pavement after each accepted update. Then reconsider every remaining
cycle corridor, including former forest roads. An iterative bridge check excludes
indispensable connectors before bounded atomic rerouting, refreshed after every
accepted removal so newly indispensable links do not waste route search. Favor fewer unique
evidenced edges, more corridor bends, longer redundant geometry, canonical keys.

All affected routes must remain within original0.2 urban distance bounds and must
not gain bends relative to their current path. No new pavement, ports or semantic
edges; every accepted deletion strictly reduces the existing road set. Rebuild
memberships and junctions from final complete paths. This avoids treating the first
forest as immutable geography; buildings/districts remain immutable. Stop safely
at 5000 alignment attempts, 1200 corridor attempts, 2M search states per phase or
100k labels per search; report truncation. Derived `roadContinuity` metadata
identifies `road-continuity/0.1` and
records both phases, exact improvements and bounds. Previous options stay intact.
Validate real output and report modest gains honestly; no curved/roundabout claim.

### Opt-in block collector planning

The next experiment replans local roads together rather than deleting strokes
one at a time. Within a bounded directory footprint, connect its actual road
terminals (building ports and boundary crossings) by a shared collector tree.
Try deterministic demand-ranked roots on an obstacle-checked lattice. New local
pavement is allowed only with real complete-route membership; unsupported or
isolated buildings gain no invented relationship. Retain required cross-links
by keeping the old path for any route the tree cannot serve within the approved
original0.2 urban distance and +4-bend limits. Thus the final road network may
retain cycles; report retained cross-link routes. Bounds never compound.

Keep buildings, ownership, doors, access spurs and boundary crossings fixed.
This is a separate opt-in derived routing experiment, not a UCG/City schema or
default layout change. Atomic acceptance requires less total pavement and no
increase in junctions or nearby parallel pavement. Rebuild segment membership
and full routes for picking and Synthetic; rollback the whole local proposal
on failure. Canonical inputs must remain byte-identical. Report attempted,
accepted and rejected plans and explicit resource caps; do not claim global
optimality or roundabouts. Validate collision using full pavement width and
compare both large repositories at identical cameras, plus sparse/unknown input.

### Road-first frontage trial

Following the maintainer's approval to continue with road/block connectors first,
add a separate frontage experiment. Keep every building/district and public
UCG/City field fixed. A visual door is derived geometry, not a code fact: allow
choosing a different obstacle-clear face/port of a building, with all incident
routes sharing that door consistently. Prefer an existing demand-backed local
collector before connecting the building to it, rather than freezing each old
port and trying to erase its indispensable branches afterward.

Only a short local approach may be replanned inside the owning footprint plus
a 7-unit boundary street margin. The rest of each relation stays intact. Demand
means unique evidenced edge IDs, never measured traffic. Keep semantic kinds
separate and include every incident relation in an atomic building proposal.
Reject proposals that cannot reconnect all endpoints, cross buildings, add loops,
or exceed the ORIGINAL0.2 bound: max(floor(1.5*N), N+4)*3.5 plus original access
spur lengths. Count the new access length inside that unchanged world-distance
budget, and retain original complete-path bends+4. Do not reset bounds at new
doors or compound them across previous consolidation passes.

Require less physical pavement and no increase in junctions or nearby parallel
length; unchanged buildings and routes keep their previous geometry. New local
links are emitted only if used by real complete paths. Report changed doors,
collector IDs/geometry, accepted/rejected candidates, bounded search, and exact
route/evidence coverage. This first frontage trial does not relocate parcels,
change canonical geography, or implement curved roads/roundabouts. It is not the
default. Verify real before/after viewers, full-width collisions, source tracing,
Synthetic, repeatability, unsupported fallback and all original distance budgets.

The first implementation ranks buildings by the length of their exclusive
dead-end approach, then unique-edge demand and stable ID; freeze that priority
before editing. Use existing straight local collector runs of at least two
lattice steps and four unique edges; try at most two demand-ranked runs near
each alternative face. Replan within 21 world units around the building and
the owning footprint's 7-unit street margin. The bounded heading-aware search
costs 80 per collector step, 100 per other existing road step, 130 per new step,
plus 30 per turn. These are planning preferences, not traffic weights.
Every accepted approach must actually touch the selected collector and use at
most 12 lattice steps before rejoining its old complete route. Final world
distance/bends are checked independently. Search is a heuristic, not an
exhaustive proof of infeasibility. Cap 600 proposals and 1M popped states;
retain safe old geometry on failure. Mixed-kind building frontages and endpoints
with zero through-road steps are protected in this first slice. Derived
`roadFrontage` metadata uses `road-frontage/0.1` in visual-access/0.3 and records
planning-time collector demand, changed doors and original-bound preservation.
Earlier-stage diagnostics describe their own intermediate geometry, not the final
frontage result. Default builds and all existing experiment flags remain unchanged.

### Shared block gateways after frontage planning

The next opt-in `--gateways` trial keeps the frontage result's doors, buildings,
districts and semantic identities fixed. Group nearby same-kind road crossings
on the same outward-facing directory boundary. Choose one existing crossing as
a shared gateway, then jointly replan local portions of all affected relations.
Prefer existing pavement; allow obstacle-certified local connectors within the
bounded patch when needed to join otherwise disconnected approaches. The first
existing-pavement-only trial joined only one group in each real city, because
the two sides of nearby crossings often had no local connecting street.
The candidate search excludes other crossings
in the group; routes unable to use the selected gateway within ORIGINAL0.2
world-distance/+4-bend limits retain their necessary old cross-links.

Accept the whole proposal only when actual used boundary crossings and total
pavement strictly decrease, with no increase in junctions, nearby parallel
pavement or total bends. Sharing a gateway creates no new logical reachability.
Rebuild exact per-route memberships for preview, selection and Synthetic; never
draw an unused reservation. Do not move endpoints, add a road through a building,
compound detour bounds, or claim an optimal/curved/roundabout network.

Use bounded directory groups and local searches; record selected gateways,
retained cross-links, original-bound checks and resource caps. Default modes and
public schemas remain unchanged. Test joint rerouting, opposite direction/kind
isolation, unsafe-detour fallback, determinism, sparse/unknown inputs and full
width clearance; inspect both real cities under identical cameras.

Implementation `block-gateways/0.1` inside visual-access/0.3 considers directories
with 4–96 descendant files, smaller first (stable ID tie-break), at most256
districts. Same-normal crossings within21 world units form triples/pairs from
the nearest8 candidates. Deduplicate identical ownership/group proposals.
Plan in a28-unit-radius square, try up to3 demand-ranked existing gateways,
and stop at480 patches,240 connected proposals or1M popped search states.
This deliberately limited ordering is not a global road optimum.

For each gateway grow a heading-aware fan on each side, excluding all other
boundary crossings. Existing steps cost100, new clear local connectors130,
turns200. These are geometric preferences, not measured traffic. Use clearance
at least0.47 and certified width min(5.5,2*(clearance-0.12)); midpoint ownership
checks protect polygon notches. A path must remain simple and within original
world-distance/+4-bend bounds; otherwise retain its original path. Only actual
used new connectors are exported. The heuristic can miss another feasible path.
`retainedRoutes` counts affected routes left unchanged, including those already
using the selected gateway; it is NOT a count of failed or indispensable routes.
Final removed-crossing counts are checked against all surviving memberships.
All earlier diagnostic blocks describe their own intermediate stages.

bend bounds, identities, memberships and access-only trips. Remove pavement only

### First reservation pilot (historical)

`python -m scripts.avenue_reservation <parcel-build> --out <new-directory>`
reserves the full 2.5-unit avenue surface plus the
existing 0.12-unit clearance margin on each side of an evidenced straight run.
It accepts only existing experimental parcel scenes. Inside one leaf directory,
move at most eight obstructing buildings outward by at most 7 world units each;
never move district boundaries or unrelated buildings. Preserve through-road
centerlines and ports, reconnect only the moved buildings' straight access spurs,
and recertify width against actual obstacles. A reservation without actual route
membership is not drawn as a road. No new schema fields/enums or default changes.

Reject candidates that cannot fit ownership, collide with buildings/other roads,
lose an endpoint, introduce a turn, or fail to clear the whole reserved run.
Compare exact route identities and through-paths, reporting access-distance
changes separately (bounded by endpoint movement). This first slice widens an
existing alignment; it does not yet straighten curves or relocate intersections.
Record bounded candidate search and explicit fallback. Same-structure hints lock
all positions; membership/district changes require an explicit cold experiment.
The developer decision report and manifest identify `avenue-reservation/0.1`
within the existing experimental parcel engine, separate from default geography.
Candidate footprints include endpoint clearance, not only lateral clearance.
Clip runs to one footprint rectangle, reject partially widened boundary segments,
and require continuous evidence-backed membership across the entire chosen run.
Try at most 64 candidates, longest first then fewer blockers and canonical keys;
stop at the first valid improvement. This is a bounded feasibility pilot, not a
global optimizer. All original building dimensions and semantic attributes stay
fixed. Recompute canonical aggregate network paths for the moved coordinates;
the inspected file-level through-paths and their provenance remain unchanged.

`--previous-scene` accepts the same-structure experimental hint policy from

not promise to retain an old reservation when its supporting demand changes.

can choose a different local candidate; default CLI/Action geography is untouched.

0.875 each. Through-route turns and junctions stay unchanged. OJ access spurs

Check deterministic metrics, duplicate-edge handling, unknown major membership,
