# ADR-020 — Structural streets with evidenced dependency demand

Status: user-approved experiment, 2026-10-18; not the default renderer or layout.

## Decision and scope

The user approved separating the physical street infrastructure from logical
dependency paths after reviewing the small gains of ADR-019 refinement.
Stop further micro-optimization of that pipeline for this experiment. Work on
`experiment/street-grid`; preserve the default, canonical UCG/City and legacy
viewers. Do not merge the experiment into main without a reviewed comparison.

This narrowly amends technical specification section7, FR-007/AC-006 and
DEC-011 **in the explicit experimental mode only**: a street may exist without
a dependency. Streets are non-semantic, deterministic derivations of directory
hierarchy and building geography. Their existence does not assert a call,
runtime communication, deployment, road traffic or missing relationship.
The public City networks still contain only evidenced edges. No new UCG edge,
entity, schema field or stable ID is introduced by this decision.

Dependencies are assigned to that infrastructure after it is built. Their
exact source, target, type, edge IDs and complete paths remain inspectable.
Static dependency demand may emphasize existing road surfaces; it is not
measured traffic. Synthetic vehicles remain explicitly illustrative and follow
stored complete paths, never arbitrary turns at a shared junction.

## Initial vertical slice

1. Select bounded directory-owned blocks and derive a sparse orthogonal street
   skeleton from geography only, with obstacle clearance. Connect building
   entrances to it once per building, independently of relation count.
2. Assign real relationships to shared streets. Keep original trip budgets in
   the first experiment. If a street route cannot satisfy them, preserve its
   original evidenced path as an explicitly counted legacy fallback; never
   silently drop a relation, claim full street coverage or invent a bridge.
3. Render structural streets separately from dependency demand and selected
   exact paths. Preserve the network Inspector and relation filters. Disclose
   coverage/fallbacks, and mark structural infrastructure in legend/SVG metadata.
4. Generate identical-camera comparisons on cold/hinted GJ and GamjaOJ plus
   small Spring and unsupported fixtures. No simultaneous lighting, skyline,
   terrain, river, curve, elevated-highway or railway redesign in this slice.

## Derived contract and guards

Experimental routing may carry `streetGrid` (`structural-streets/0.1`) with
non-semantic geometry, hierarchy provenance, budgets, route assignments and
fallback diagnostics. Existing `visual-access/0.3` route records retain their
meaning. Consumers must explicitly opt into the structural layer. Old modes
must remain byte-compatible where code/configuration is unchanged.

Resource caps and deterministic tie-breaks are mandatory. Reject malformed
geography, prevent full-width building intersections, retain all logical IDs,
and never execute target code or install target dependencies. A capped or
disconnected search is unknown/fallback, not proof of no source relationship.

## Acceptance before promotion

- Repeated structural geometry identical even if source dependency edges change
  while canonical directory/building geography remains fixed.
- Every exported relationship retains exact provenance; street junction sharing
  does not imply transitive or additional semantic connections.
- No building movement, no full-width intersections, no silent omissions.
- Both renderer families consume the same streets and assigned route data;
  actual browser/preview screenshots and Synthetic cache checks are inspected.
- Report street coverage, fallback count, detours, occupied surface length,
  intersections and rendering cost alongside the visual comparison.
- Treat an unattractive/disconnected pilot as evidence to improve, not a reason
  to silently change the default or relax approved route budgets.

## Opt-in aligned blocks, 2026-10-18

Continuation remains inside the approved fixed-geography experiment. The
`--aligned-blocks` variant uses `structural-streets/0.2` derived metadata, not a
new public City/UCG schema. It removes the half-cell offset of block seed lines:
in the current seven-unit parcels, that offset puts many lines through buildings
instead of the gaps. The same obstacle/width checks still govern every segment.

Disconnected seed components grow simultaneous geometry-only wavefronts. Keep
one connection per pair of neighboring waves, including alternate local loops
instead of only a spanning tree. This is bounded, deterministic adjacency, not
traffic demand or a claim of globally optimal roads. Use the existing2M
infrastructure search cap; partial exploration must remain explicit.

No building moves and no entrance reorientation are part of this variant.
Original doors, relationships and complete-trip budgets stay fixed. Preserve
the old unaligned option for controlled comparisons and do not promote either
to the default build. Compare all-trip pavement as well as assigned-only views.

## Structural surface continuity, 2026-10-18

Aligned presentation may narrow renderer-only pavement inside its original
clearance envelope to avoid abrupt short-wide slabs. Suppress short bulges and
taper sustained transitions; do not move centerlines or rewrite relations.
All renderer families share derived polygons. This changes neither topology
nor assignment; original0.1 rendering stays intact. The separate safe-width
demand overlay can remain rectangular; do not claim fully unified road geometry.
