# ADR-021 — Non-orthogonal structural street reconstruction

Status: user-approved experimental direction, 2026-10-18. Not a default-mode
promotion, public City/UCG migration, or authorization to merge main.

The user explicitly requested diagonal roads and naturally angled junctions,
not more rounding of an axis-aligned network. Extend ADR-020 with an opt-in
`--diagonal-streets` experiment. Keep buildings and all source evidence fixed.

First slice: reconstruct branch-free street chains as clearance-certified
straight chords between their fixed terminals. Terminals include all branches,
building entrances and complete relationship endpoints. Split the complete
structural/access geometry at crossings before identifying chains. Do not remove
an intervening access point, invent a crossing, bridge a building or fabricate
a relation. Keep the old chain if shortening or full-width clearance fails.

Render real diagonal pavement with perpendicular cross-sections and bounded
junction joins. Do not apply the rejected broad deformation field to adjacent
roads. Preserve logical segment/route IDs and membership; expose derived physical
polylines and paint so CPU preview, WebGL, Canvas, picking and Synthetic agree.
Logical baseline paths remain available for provenance/length comparisons;
physical paths must have identical complete-trip endpoints and connectivity.

The derived plan is `street-diagonals/0.1`, separate from public schemas.
This first mode is mutually exclusive with local corner curves, avoiding two
independent competing geometry maps. All caps fail explicitly. Geographic
candidate widths come from structural streets, not evidence IDs or edge counts.
Existing access geometry and trip endpoints may veto a candidate for safety;
this is not permission to infer new streets from relationship volume.

Evaluate sparse/unknown, medium/modular and dense inputs, including the user's
SQLite Browser example. Report eligible/rejected/replaced chains, lengths,
building/road collisions, route identity/continuity, repeated bytes and actual
same-camera captures. A diagonal solution may remain sparse in dense layouts.
Y-shaped angles can result from changed approach directions, but this is not
arbitrary junction relocation, global street synthesis, roundabouts or a claim
that every scene has escaped the grid. Those require separate verified slices.

Implementation checkpoint 2026-10-19: explicit arrangement splits crossings and
collinear overlaps before extracting chains. Candidates need at least 10%
shortening and a 7..280 world-unit chord, with displacement on both axes.
Preserve each terminal mouth; transition over at most one third of the chord
toward its widest original structural width. Try that envelope, the midpoint
of original width bounds, then the narrow bound, certifying each complete strip
and convex mouth join. Do not collapse a long road to its shortest throat width.
Unchanged neighboring streets and their width profiles are not deformed.

The plan does not delete logical intermediate segment identities. Their physical
positions are interpolated along the chord; complete trip endpoints stay fixed.
Stored width stations and physical paint fragments are shared by consumers.
Input/atomic edges, spatial indexing, collision checks, mapping and paint samples
have explicit limits. HTTP/messaging and door-access chains are not candidates.
See STREET_GRID_PLAN and QUALITY_LOG for the four-repository comparison and
remaining dense-grid limitations. This remains opt-in on experiment/street-grid.
