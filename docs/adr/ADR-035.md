# ADR-035 — Occasional alleys through existing long frontage rows

Status: implemented as an opt-in policy; baseline comparison recorded 2026-10-19.

## Intent

The accepted shared-junction city should gain occasional permeability through
long building rows, not another dense cross-grid. Preserve all building frames,
source ownership, mixed neighborhoods and parent patterns. Do not move buildings
or paint disconnected decorative roads to manufacture a result.

## Experiment

Add explicit `--street-policy permeable`, extending shared junction policy after
unchanged lot planning. Record experimental generator
`flat-adaptive-neighborhoods/0.5`. Original0.3 and shared0.4 remain reproducible;
all three may share geography hints. City/UCG contracts and source IDs do not
change. Only the experimental generator enum gains a compatible value.

Find existing gaps in sufficiently long, locally aligned frontage rows. Require
several actual buildings on both sides of the proposed cut. Join the row's road
to an existing non-driveway street across the block, inside the same principal
district. Use a narrow full-width corridor with clearance from **whole parcels**,
not merely building centers. Never cut through a building, its parcel or a
driveway; avoid existing road intersections and redundant nearby cross-links.

Bound length, candidate/search/index work and spatial frequency. Retain stable
ID-based ordering; cap additions per neighborhood and globally, with wide
separation between accepted cuts. If no safe useful gap exists, add nothing.
Budget exhaustion retains only certified additions and discloses the limit.

Atomize actual new junctions into the physical street graph and reroute the
same evidenced trips over it. An alley is structural infrastructure, not a new
dependency or measured traffic. Unsupported repositories may have alleys but
must retain zero invented trips. Recompute decorative clearance against the
new real pavement. On routing failure retain the certified shared baseline.

## Verification

Positive long-row/gap fixture; short rows, blocked lots, nearby cross streets,
frequency and exhausted-budget negatives; deterministic bytes and complete
frames after hinted edits. Compare GJ/OJ/unknown corpus with identical cameras
in CPU/WebGL/Canvas. Verify full-width parcel/entrance clearance, genuine
two-ended connectivity, complete source trip identities and unchanged old
modes. Report actual alley counts/lengths and visual restraint, not a claimed
whole-city transformation. Existing material/LOD/terrain work remains open.

## Observed first result

`sparse-alleys-r1`: GJ2/PocketBase2 cuts, OJ0/SQLite0, all3175 whole frames,
principal patterns and original source memberships retained. GJ31 original trips
shorten; other repositories retain their original route geometry. An actual
pointer-selected GJ dependency uses the new alley:192.12→70.48 units with exact
original edge evidence. CPU/WebGL/Canvas share real physical pavement; greenery
respects it. See CURRENT_BASELINE for thresholds and QUALITY_LOG for commands,
renderer checks, reproducibility, limitations and delivery.
