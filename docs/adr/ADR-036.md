# ADR-036 — Terrain before urban allocation, not scenery over a fixed city

Status: opt-in terrain-first implementation, 2026-10-19; flat baseline retained.

## Direction

The maintainer explicitly rejects freezing the accepted flat city and adding
natural objects afterwards. Follow World Generation Plan sections5/7/9/10:
estimate demand, establish global terrain, then allocate land, roads and parcels.
Terrain can move buildings and expand the city; it cannot delete source truth.
Flat outputs remain a reproducible ablation, not mandatory coordinates for the
new mode. This does not replace City/UCG or the existing flat engine.

## Bounded first slice

Add opt-in `--terrain lake-district` for adaptive neighborhoods. Before district
parcel planning, create seeded shared lake and hill reserves from the initial
world envelope, independently of district roles, roads and empty lots. Persist
terrain/0.1 geometry/seed and protected footprints. Keep initial terrain fixed
during capacity repairs. Compatible hints reuse it; flat hints are explicitly
incompatible. Never relocate a lake to accommodate an existing building.

Planning grammars remain reference fields. Clip affected roads out of buffered
natural reserves and connect them to common shoreline/contour roads. Filter
complete parcels and frontage against terrain and adapted roads BEFORE source
assignment. If capacity is inadequate, repartition and expand in bounded repair
rounds, without GRID substitution or lost files. The same terrain spans all
administrative boundaries.

This mode has closed lakes and low-poly hills with flat buildable plains. It is
not drainage simulation, rivers, city-wide graded foundations, bridges or tunnels.
Choose land detours, not fake roads across water. Nature is environmental
presentation, never a technology/source relationship.

## Compatibility and consumers

Add compatible experimental generator enum `terrain-adaptive/0.1`, optional
`terrain` and `terrainPreset:lake-district`; retain old meanings/default bytes.
Planning0.5 accepts the same new preset; contour main roads retain parent
pattern IDs and unique metadata identities while physically sharing geometry.
Terrain records contain explicit shared faces, masks and versioned fields.
Canonical World drives CPU/WebGL/Canvas, including Structure inspection. Keep
canonical City/UCG and source IDs unchanged; new World positions intentionally
differ. Preserve real role mixing and exact trips. Planting is last and avoids
water, reserved slopes and actual roads.

## Gates

Verify terrain is generated without consulting parcels/roads, before urban
placement. Assert actual displacement, protected land exclusion, full-width road
clearance, complete access/trips, bounded repairs, repeated bytes and hinted
small edits. Test a forced arterial reserve, sparse/unknown and pinned GJ/OJ/
PocketBase/SQLite. Inspect actual Overview/Detail/CPU/Canvas/Synthetic and source
evidence. Record area tradeoffs and the flat-plain limitation honestly.

## First comparison

Pinned corpus retains3175 source buildings and5782 exact source trips. GJ/OJ
areas grow13.4%/4.4%; PocketBase retains outer area, SQLite grows31.1%. The
initial mode switch relocates many buildings, not just ones touching water;
existing local grammar and capacity allocation can repartition whole neighborhoods.
Compatible natural hints retain48 old full frames after49th file and fixed
terrain. Initial flat-to-natural migration is not claimed to be spatially stable.
