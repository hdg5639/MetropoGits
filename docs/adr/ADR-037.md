# ADR-037 — Connected river corridor with real shared crossings

Status: implementation slice authorized by the terrain-first plan and next-task
request, 2026-10-19. Opt-in only; no replacement of City/UCG or lake-district.

## Outcome

Add `--terrain river-corridor`, terrain/0.2, terrain-adaptive/0.2. Generate a
single globally connected, seeded meandering channel and terminal water bodies
before urban allocation. Use an explicit analytical channel with monotone
downstream stationing, not independent noise-selected puddles. This is a bounded
topological water prototype, **not** a drainage/flow solver or a graded city-wide
height field. Water remains level and buildable plains remain flat. World Plan
section7's full hydrology/elevation work remains open.

Reserve the union of water/banks before roads and parcels. Trace the exposed
outer bank of that union, not a separate road loop around every channel tile.
Predetermine a small number of transverse crossings independent of source roles
or existing roads. Shared bank roads and bridge approaches precede source
assignment; capacity repairs move/expand the city, never move the river.

## Real bridges, not props

Bridge approaches join the same physical graph as bank roads. Deck profiles
rise from ground-height abutments, clear water and return to ground at the other
bank; bounded grades and full-width clearance are validated. Reserve complete
crossings against parcels. Ordinary streets must not cross protected terrain.
Only physical pieces geometrically certified against a declared crossing may
cross water. Keep crossing identity and per-endpoint heights after atomization,
sharing and rerouting. No invented source edge or traffic quota.

CPU, WebGL and Canvas consume the same deck/approach geometry. Selection,
hit-testing, demand markings and Synthetic use the same interpolated heights;
Synthetic retains its exact source/target trip, never chooses a random turn.
Crossings remain structural even when no relationship uses them. Unsupported
languages must not gain fabricated traffic merely because a bridge exists.

## Compatibility and gates

Only compatible additions to experimental World/planning contracts. Existing
flat/lake versions, seeds, defaults and bytes remain unchanged. River hints
reuse the original terrain and compatible complete source frames; different
terrain presets reject hints explicitly. Bound feature counts, clipping work,
crossing count and validation work; fail explicitly on infeasible supply.

Check maintained Spring input and pinned OJ/GJ/PocketBase/SQLite corpora, actual
Overview/close-up/CPU/Canvas, shared bridge source evidence and elevated
Synthetic with no static rebuilds. Repeat canonical packages, test small edits,
reject forged crossings/heights and ordinary roads through water. Record city
expansion/replanning costs; no claim that this completes hydrology or density.
