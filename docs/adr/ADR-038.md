# ADR-038 — Shared graded ground, street profiles and level foundations

Status: implemented and verified as an opt-in bounded slice, 2026-10-19.
`river-valley`; old flat/lake/river modes remain unchanged. This is not approval
of a new default or completion of the full terrain plan.

## Scope

The river prototype has real crossings but all buildable land is flat. Add a
seeded global continuous upland field BEFORE parcel supply is accepted. Reuse
the connected river/crossing geography as the low, level valley floor. The
field has one world-space triangulated lattice, never separate district noise
or unrelated per-building elevations. Persist immutable field parameters in
terrain/0.3, generator terrain-adaptive/0.3. Geometry beyond the initial envelope
uses the same field during bounded urban capacity repairs.

Planning rejects complete candidate lots with excessive relief before assigning
sources. Buildings retain original width/depth/height/archetype/evidence, sit on
level raised foundations, and expose their ground/foundation heights. No slanted
code buildings, floating foundations or silently omitted files. A new preset
may repartition urban land; compatible hinted edits preserve the field and
complete existing frames where feasible.

## Physical continuity and consumers

Street profiles follow the exact same piecewise-planar field and record all
lattice/diagonal breakpoints. Bridge profiles keep their fixed certified grades
and meet level banks. Driveways meet the building platform and street height;
reject excessive grades or discontinuous junctions. Source XZ paths/IDs remain
exact logical trips; routing may use physical 3D lengths without changing facts.

Final land tessellation is derived from that fixed field and current urban
envelope, not a rerolled terrain. CPU/WebGL/Canvas consume identical explicit
ground/pavement/foundation geometry. Whole meshes, source picking, camera fit,
labels, aggregate representations, trees, selected paths and Synthetic follow
the same elevation data. No visual-only lifting that leaves traffic underground.

Add compatible optional fields/enums only to experimental World/planning;
canonical City/UCG and old mode bytes/meanings stay intact. Resource bounds on
field extent, tessellation, sampling/profiles and existing planning/routing
still apply. Exhaustion/infeasible capacity is explicit.

## Non-goals and proof

This is gentle buildable relief, not full watershed hydrology, earthworks cost
optimization, steep switchbacks, tunnels or arbitrary slope accommodation.
Do not hide density/road-layout defects under noise or change role mixing.

Verify grade/pad/ground contact, shared border heights, bridge/driveway joins,
exact source conservation, actual uphill/downhill Synthetic, determinism,
hinted growth, negative payloads and limits. Run fresh maintained input and
pinned GJ/OJ/PocketBase/SQLite; inspect real Overview/close-ups/CPU/Canvas.

## Observed delivery

All four pinned corpora retain source City/UCG bytes, neighborhoods/roles and
exact file-pair trips. Complete candidate lots are rejected before assignment;
urban area grows by about4.0/7.7/14.0/10.5 percent (OJ/GJ/PocketBase/SQLite),
so this is not a density improvement. Preset migration relocates buildings;
compatible one-file growth retains48 existing complete parcel frames.

Matched actual WebGL views, forced Canvas and CPU previews are recorded at
`artifacts/visual/valley-focus-r3/index.html` and related Quality Log entries.
Actual Synthetic mesh heights follow selected uphill roads, with no static
geometry rebuilds during the measured animation. Relief is intentionally gentle
and subtle at Overview, not a dramatic landscape or hydrology simulation.
