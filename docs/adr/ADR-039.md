# ADR-039 — Open rivers, tributaries and conditional mountain crossings

Status: implemented and verified as a bounded opt-in extension, 2026-10-19,
following the user's terrain correction. Existing flat/lake/river/valley
artifacts stay valid; this does not complete general hydrology or tunneling.

## Correction and scope

The accepted river ends in two pools inside the city. That is a closed elongated
lake, not a convincing river corridor. Add `river-landscape`, terrain/0.4 and
terrain-adaptive/0.4, without changing source City/UCG or older generator bytes.
Before urban allocation, reserve an open main channel and a smaller connected
tributary, plus elongated mountain reserves where feasible. Channel ends extend
beyond the bounded repair domain; no terminal pools. Rendering clips natural
geometry to the actual city boundary, never relocates water to fit buildings.

The global graded field includes the shared water network. Urban expansion must
still leave upstream/downstream outside. Ordinary roads and complete parcels
avoid protected water/mountains; certified shared bridges remain valid.
Branches share water topology, not fabricated semantic relationships.

## Mountains and tunnels

Generate bounded candidate passes through a mountain before source assignment.
Only construct a candidate tunnel when an actual evidenced file-pair trip uses
it; unused candidate infrastructure is not drawn as a completed tunnel. Preserve
original endpoints/edge IDs. Certify full spans, widths, grade, mountain cover and
water exclusion independently. Rendering must cut genuine portal openings and
retain the same physical profile for selection and Synthetic, not draw an arch
against a sealed hillside. Unsupported repositories may have mountains and
structural detours but no invented tunnel traffic.

## Delivery gates and exclusions

Use actual GJ/OJ plus low-semantic and maintained sparse input; inspect CPU,
WebGL and Canvas. Check whole-width clearance, all source/role/neighborhood
membership and exact trips, deterministic repeats, growth hints, old snapshots,
resource limits and forged crossings. Record relocation/land costs honestly.

This is an analytical terrain network, not rainfall/drainage simulation or a
general civil-engineering solver. Keep prior role mixing, planning grammars,
density/material/LOD backlog and static-analysis boundaries intact. Do not
spend the iteration on negligible cosmetic changes.

## Observed result

All four maintained corpus cities have an open main river, one tributary and
one feasible ridge. Actual OJ source routing constructs one tunnel, used by a
test-file→production-file reference; GJ/PocketBase/SQLite do not construct their
unused candidates. Source buildings, roles, neighborhood membership and exact
trip identities are conserved. Independent repeated generation/package bytes,
all entrance access, schema and physical-clearance checks pass.

Same-mode hinted growth preserves48 old frames. Preset migration is broader:
680/1324/654/491 buildings relocate (OJ/GJ/PocketBase/SQLite), with urban area
ratios1.342/1.031/1.018/1.000 against the valley baseline. This is not a density
improvement. Real matched views and actual tunnel pointer/Synthetic checks:
`artifacts/visual/river-landscape-focus-r1/index.html`; details in QUALITY_LOG.
