# ADR-041 — Physical street hierarchy without changing source trips

Status: user-authorized Road Network and Campus Planning continuation,
2026-10-19; first RC-00/01 vertical slice. CAMPUS construction remains next.

## Outcome and scope

Freeze the current porous/terrain-first corpus. Classify its actual streets as
ARTERIAL (shared inter-district corridors), COLLECTOR (reserved backbone and
necessary connections), LOCAL (ordinary frontage) or SERVICE (existing building
access). These are structural functions, not HTTP/traffic/research claims.
PEDESTRIAN is reserved for real future paths; do not relabel roads as walks.

Classify offline from exact physical geometry/topology and reserved plans.
Close gaps in the coarse *presentation hierarchy* along existing shortest
street paths, never across empty land. Report physical connected components,
source access and port attachments, including disconnected/unknown cases.
No physical road, building, parcel, terrain or dependency may move or disappear.

At overview, emphasize a connected arterial/collector backbone and subdue
minor pavement/access. District/detail progressively restore local contrast.
Do not delete minor streets or animate cars over invisible infrastructure.
Separate physical markings from dependency demand; avoid repainting the same
road once per relation. Selection and explicit All routes retain complete exact
source paths, source evidence, quantitative demand and own-path Synthetic.

## Contract and consumers

Add explicit `--street-hierarchy` to experimental World generation. Optional
`streetPlan`, pinned as `street-plan/0.1`, contains classifications, component/
frontage/port audits and work counters. It is additive post-planning metadata;
geometry generator versions/IDs and canonical UCG/City stay unchanged. Flag off
retains prior canonical output. Geography hints can cross this presentation-only
flag since planning is identical. Schema and independent reconstruction reject
forged/incomplete metadata. Never guess fake connections after a work limit.

Producers/consumers: world generator/validation/schema, planning export, CPU
showcase, static browser data, shared viewer policy, WebGL/Canvas and inspector.
Physical street class is distinct from existing edge-count demand grades.
All geometry/class selection is prepared offline, not rebuilt each frame.

## Acceptance and restraint

Test shared boundaries, local frontage/access, real coarse connections, missing
ports/components, permutation determinism and explicit resource bounds. Compare
GJ/OJ/PocketBase/SQLite and small Hanul/maintained fixture with unchanged source
graphs, world geometry, original trip identities and exact canonical repeat runs.
Inspect actual README, three WebGL LODs, Canvas, selected complete routes and
Synthetic static builds0. Check small hinted edits and previous golden outputs.

Do not claim the entire proposal or CAMPUS is finished. CP-00/01 must later add
evidence-bounded weighted eligibility, reserved shared spaces and real connected
walkable access; green paint or a directory called lab is not a campus.
