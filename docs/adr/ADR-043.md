# ADR-043 — Capacity-bounded garden-cluster campus morphology

Status: approved road/campus design continuation, 2026-10-19; CP-02 first slice.
Not all campus variants, emergency access or a materials overhaul.

## Decision

Add explicit `--campus-style varied`, requiring the existing `--campus` opt-in.
Default `quad` retains campus-plan/0.1 and its canonical geography. The varied
mode adds campus-plan/0.2 with style `varied`; UCG/City, original source IDs,
sizes, roles and parent/zone ownership do not change. Cross-style geography
hints are incompatible; same-style hints retain feasible original addresses.

Keep the existing evidence gate and eligibility seed. Choose between actual
ACADEMIC_QUAD and TECH_PARK forms with a separate zone-local seeded preference,
then enforce physical fit. No unconditional role-to-form mapping, mandatory new
campus in an unsupported repository, fake source facility or new dependency.

TECH_PARK means two small reserved garden courts, two groups of real files
facing connected walking loops, a short offset promenade between them and an
actual outside-street connection. Its slots, whole rotated parcels, entrances,
lawns and walking links are one offline plan. Avoid the previous one-large-lawn
stencil; do not spawn decorative asphalt or a second city-wide pattern layer.
The name describes physical planning, not verified technological/research use.

Try the preferred form inside the existing allocation, then the alternate form.
Insufficient capacity, whole-parcel/entrance clearance, unsafe terrain or bounded
connectivity retains the original local grammar with explicit diagnostics. Do
not inflate global land, erase protected macro roads or discard original files.
Preserve the original capacity-repair order and source-mixed membership logic.

## Consumers and compatibility

Generator/adaptive planner, independent reconstruction, World/planning schemas,
CLI/hint compatibility and both inspectors recognize the pinned optional style.
CPU/WebGL/Canvas already consume the common source parcels, physical walkways
and reserved gardens; no per-frame planning, meshing or pathfinding is added.
Street classes and original-pair Synthetic retain their current meaning.

Older campus-plan/0.1 stays valid and reproducible. Inspectors disclose the
actual selected variant and original members. New derived slot/physical IDs are
versioned local geography, never a rewrite of canonical UCG identities.

## Acceptance and limits

Test compact/blocked gardens, all whole parcels and entrance ribbons, terrain
grade, original-source retention, seeded choice/alternate fallback, forged
metadata, schemas, deterministic bytes and hinted growth. Reuse existing
candidate/geometry/output budgets; do not increase caps to force a variant.

Run maintained fixture input through the real CLI and CPU/static viewer. Compare
GJ/OJ/PocketBase/SQLiteBrowser and sparse unknown input with matched cameras and
LOD, including close views of actual selected forms. Record changes in selected
zones and unrelated geography separately. If the only change is a label or lawn
color, the morphology slice has not met its visible acceptance criterion.

Riverside/terrain-specific variants and cross-boundary transitions remain open.
