# ADR-045 — Whole-neighborhood campus planning, not garden pockets

2026-10-19. User explicitly rejected the pocket interpretation and authorized
the original Road Network & Campus Planning intent: an integrated walkable
compound of related building groups, not eight buildings around a lawn.

## Correction and compatibility

ADR-042/043 are bounded prototypes, not fulfillment of CAMPUS acceptance.
Preserve them for reproducibility. New explicit `--campus-style neighborhood`
uses optional `campus-plan/0.3`; no UCG/City schema, identity, role, dimension or
source-evidence change. It supersedes the pocket fit rule only in this mode.

## Planning boundary

- Keep terrain-first reserves, parent ownership, neighborhood membership/bounds
  and protected major/inter-zone streets. Replace the selected neighborhood's
  ordinary local address/road plan BEFORE assigning any source file.
- Plan two or three shared activity courts, a connected pedestrian promenade,
  shorter frontage wings and actual exterior access. Reserve open spaces and
  circulation before whole rotated parcels. Do not paint another vehicle grid.
- All original neighborhood members participate. No eight-member quota, leftover
  ordinary sub-neighborhood, forced research identity or fake utility building.
  Actual relation affinity guides source groups; roles must remain mixed where
  the source neighborhood is mixed. Group/anchor IDs trace to actual members.
- Existing through arterials/crossings can remain; do not misrepresent retained
  vehicle corridors as pedestrian-only. Walking links must really connect to
  the authoritative graph and retain independent evidence-path semantics.
- If full capacity, clearance, terrain grade, connected circulation or bounded
  planning fails, retain the original neighborhood with an explicit diagnostic.
  Do not silently fall back to a successful-looking eight-file garden pocket,
  shrink source buildings, drop relations or enlarge unrelated city land.

## Acceptance

Actual same-camera GJ/OJ/PocketBase/SQLite comparisons with role colours kept
constant. Include a maintained source fixture from analysis through rendering.
Confirm all campus members, at least two occupied building groups, actual courts
and walking connections, replaced local circulation, exact source/edge identity,
zero whole-parcel/court/pavement overlaps, all entrances/ports connected, fixed
terrain/parent/neighborhood polygons, unrelated geography and repeated bytes.
Test old-mode compatibility, infeasible fallback, forged metadata and same-mode
small-change hints. Inspect CPU, WebGL, Canvas, source/member drill-down and
Synthetic static-cache reuse. A visually unconvincing result remains a prototype;
test count or a new variant name does not make the campus milestone complete.
