# ADR-049 — Soft campus commons and guarded group bearings

2026-10-19. User also requests the previously deferred rectangular-court/row
monotony correction. Add optional `--campus-style landscape`, campus-plan/0.4,
without rewriting the preserved quad/varied/neighborhood modes.

Keep whole original neighborhoods, members, source anchors and role mixtures.
Use elliptical lawns inside earlier court reserves and real walking arcs in
unused rectangular corners, retaining occupied straight frontages and attaching
gates/promenades to the curved loop. Propose coordinated small
rotations of whole building groups AND their frontage walks, reconnecting real
promenades and preserving full source dimensions, original vehicle frontage,
terrain/zone ownership, capacity, grades, court and parcel clearance. Independent
one-group-at-a-time rotations can collide with an interlocked neighbor's wing;
test a bounded coordinated set instead of weakening collision requirements.

Record actual accepted `groupRotations`. Roomy inputs can have distinct local
bearings; interlocked dense inputs retain zero rotation. Do NOT describe a zero-
rotation output as a successful group-orientation refactor. All-member evidence
inspection and physical access remain mandatory; no smaller pocket substitution.

This is a guarded spatial variation, not completion of organic campus placement.
If real corpus rotations remain zero, report that limitation and prioritize a
future before-assignment redevelopment grammar, not repeated angle micro-tuning
or cosmetic effects hiding the restriction. Compare on the exact same terrain
and inspect the actual forms before claiming visual completion.
