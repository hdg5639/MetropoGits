# ADR-050 — Plan organic campus frontage before assigning sources

2026-10-19. Continue the approved campus milestone with optional
`--campus-style organic`, `campus-plan/0.5`. Dense real precincts cannot be
improved by rotating already interlocked rectilinear wings (ADR-049).

Reserve rounded commons, connected promenades and gently fanning frontage
walks BEFORE generating full-size parcels or assigning source members. Each
walk supplies tangent-facing parcels along its actual polyline, not axis-snapped
rows. The existing bounded center selection, evidence eligibility, whole-zone
membership, source-degree anchors and graph-aware assignment remain authoritative.
Keep protected vehicle corridors, terrain, neighborhood ownership and all source
and relation identities. Never shrink source parcels to make a visual fit.

This is an additive experimental planning option, not a replacement of UCG,
City or older campus modes. Reconstruct 0.5 independently through the same
deterministic geometry policy; retain older modes unchanged. On insufficient
whole-neighborhood capacity, retain the previous safe whole-precinct planner
with an explicit diagnostic; do not substitute a small successful-looking pocket.
Bound candidate paths and clearance work; do not add per-frame planning.

Acceptance: actual same-terrain GJ/OJ comparisons must show changed occupied
frontages, not only lawns. Check full membership/access, exact relation identities,
terrain and outside-campus stability, schemas, repeatability, nearby growth and
real CPU/WebGL/Canvas output. If a dense case remains unchanged, report it as a
fallback, not as completed organic campus redevelopment. This does not complete
riverside-specific planning, service/emergency semantics or architectural assets.
