# ADR-052 — Restore evidence-backed compounds in the rotated World viewer

2026-10-19 user regression report: World export marks every source as oriented;
the legacy LOD guard consequently excludes every visual aggregate. The guard
was correct for old axis-aligned geometry, but no replacement was delivered.

Keep the legacy guard. Derive bounded presentation-only role compounds offline
from actual rotated source footprints, exact rule evidence, module and physical
neighborhood ownership. Only adjacent sets of at least four equally evidenced
page/API/gate/test files may merge. A proposed oriented envelope must fit inside
the original zone without covering other parcels, actual public streets, water,
terrain reserves or campus commons. Only a member's own terminal door-access
stub may meet its compound, as it already meets the original building. Bound
clearance/index work and degrade to individual buildings on exhaustion.

Keep World/City/UCG and every original route/entrance untouched. Supply the
existing LOD/inspector with certified oriented models: same facility at all zooms,
explicit Expand constituent files, exact members/evidence and individual source
inspection. CPU/WebGL/Canvas share the same compound geometry; never simply
remove the orientation guard or draw an axis-aligned mass over a curved street.
This restores role landmarks, not generic low-information overview plates.

Verify real GJ/OJ gate/test compounds plus unknown-input non-promotion; test
mixed roles, module/zone boundaries, streets/terrain/other occupants, bounds,
determinism, filtering, expansion, source navigation and unchanged routing.
