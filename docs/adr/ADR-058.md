# ADR-058 — Local procedural surface materials and cached daylight shadows

2026-10-21. The maintainer requests a substantial visual-quality milestone,
not another planning/routing micro-optimization. Existing source geography,
campus complexes, evidence and navigation remain the input, not redesign targets.

- Enrich the existing bundled WebGL1 renderer; do not replace its core technology
  or require a remote renderer, asset CDN, AI service or target-repository build.
  Generate facade/roof/asphalt/paving/grass/rock/water patterns in the shader.
  All patterns are artistic presentation, never construction or runtime evidence.
- Use facade-local metre coordinates so rotated buildings retain aligned windows,
  mullions and floor divisions. Stable identity selects a bounded material variant;
  it does not invent a role. Existing roof-role accents, full role colours and
  source selection/filter controls retain their meaning.
- Use one bounded, optional packed-depth sun map (at most2048 square) with soft
  filtering. Cache it with the static triangle array. Camera and Synthetic ticks
  must not rebuild static geometry or the shadow map; activity is not a new caster.
  Do not disable depth testing/culling to mask broken terrain geometry.
- Rounded decorative tree crowns may replace the old angular crowns only within
  their existing reserve and height, with a bounded vertex budget. Do not add new
  tree sites or move semantic buildings/streets for the screenshot.
- Offer a keyboard-reachable Rich materials toggle. Missing derivatives use a
  bounded smoothing fallback; missing highp/framebuffer support disables shadows,
  with a complete white sampler texture. Existing Canvas fallback and CPU SVG
  remain usable; do not claim shader-detail parity in the CPU preview.
- No canonical UCG/City/Planning/World schema, identity, geometry or source-edge
  changes. Renderer-internal vertices add surface coordinates and extents; audit
  counters must use the new stride, not silently misreport triangle counts.

Validate real small/large/low-semantic corpus viewers, matched-camera material
comparisons, source/route controls, Synthetic cache behavior, optional-capability
fallbacks and canonical byte equality. Record actual images and limitations;
this is not a claim of AAA photorealistic/PBR rendering or hardware FPS.
