# ADR-061 — Program-land reservation before precinct street subdivision

2026-10-22. Follow-on to ADR-060 and the maintainer's architectural-scale request.

The first estate experiment often failed despite ample sector area. Structural
arterials already crossed its site before facility reservations were fitted.
Adding more city area does not resolve that fragmentation and moves unrelated
geography. Change the ordering in a separate opt-in `precinct` style, not the
existing default or saved estate/0.8 contract.

- Derive facility-site/circulation/common-space demand from the same bounded,
  source-affinity requests before committing structural circulation. Require
  sufficient existing developable sector area; do not expand the whole map.
- Reserve at most one eligible sector per directory parent. Keep its original
  polygon, all neighboring sector polygons and the natural terrain. Reroute
  structural arterial portions inside the reservation onto a shared perimeter;
  retain all external pieces/endpoints and reconstruct real boundary access.
- A perimeter that crosses protected nature/water is infeasible, not permission
  to bulldoze or invent a bridge. Retain working programmed morphology when
  no certified complete precinct fits. Later terrain/grade, capacity, source,
  trip and overlap validators remain mandatory and are not weakened.
- The reservation is structural/derived and explicitly not a source relation.
  Real dependency trips are generated afterwards on certified final geometry.
  Source IDs, roles, provenance, UCG/City bytes and source-size checks stay intact.
- `campus-plan/0.9`/`precinct` is additive in experimental World/Planning output.
  Facility reservations continue using the typed0.8 site representation. The
  adaptive pattern records campus-reservation/0.1, physical demand, sector ID,
  polygon, rerouted structural IDs and perimeter IDs. Independent reconstruction
  must reproduce the exact plan, including protected street identity.
- Explicitly connect required exterior components before allocating facilities,
  including complete plans. Keep source doors on facility perimeters. No late
  connector is permitted to pass through an already allocated complex.
- Do not claim arbitrary individual physical footprints, universal campus
  variants, photorealism, runtime traffic or city-wide land-growth completion.
  Compare real large-corpus positive outputs, unsupported inputs, determinism,
  source traceability, locality and measured cost before default promotion.
- An explicit compatible `--previous-world` may migrate programmed/estate
  geography into precinct mode. Reuse only its district/sector anchors and
  normal source hints; reconstruct all new circulation/reservations and disclose
  migration. Existing seed/namespace/configuration/version checks still apply.
  Cold mode changes may avoid different old capacity repairs and therefore
  change macro geography; use migration for a controlled same-site comparison.

Consumers: World generator/adaptive reconstruction, campus selection/circulation,
compound export, CLI, World/Planning schemas and actual browser/source inspector.
Existing styles retain their previous algorithms and schemas remain compatible.
