# ADR-065 — Compose height within capacity using continuous district fields

2026-10-22. Accepted interim height composition requested alongside the urban
redesign. This is NOT acceptance of land-first downtown master planning.

The uncapped0.4 compiler filled every selected form's capacity, exposing slender
towers scattered across small source-sized lots. Keep the removal of the legacy
10 ceiling, but distinguish capacity from allocated physical height.

- Catalog0.5 retains physical-height/0.1 capacity and adds height-allocation/0.1.
  A deterministic district-skyline/0.1 field composes height across existing
  functional neighborhoods, with up to3 broad elliptical centers and smooth
  transition. Coordinates derive from neighborhood geography, not reference hubs.
- District UrbanCharacterProfile influences the field amplitude only where
  verified functional roles exist. Unknown fallback is neutral even for a star
  of utility imports. No individual incoming count influences its height.
- Tall forms use28–100% of capacity according to the continuous field; medium
  forms50–100%, institutional forms70–100%, low halls88–100%. There is no new
  absolute maximum. Actual capacity scales with span. Large parcels can remain
  large/tall; this slice does not pretend small lots became broad headquarters.
- Certified campus compounds retain their existing site geometry and silhouettes,
  persistent source membership and exact member expansion. Do not modulate them
  as a bag of unrelated file towers. Individual retained representations receive
  the field when explicitly expanded.
- Independent validation reconstructs both capacity and allocation from the
  original World/UCG, checks every part/mesh vertex, and preserves all XZ geometry.
  CPU/WebGL share the compiled physical height, including camera/picking/shadows.
- Add checksum-covered skyline-plan.json, compact inspector data and explicit
  Canvas/WebGL disclosures. Canonical UCG/City/World/Planning/world-view0.2 and
  noncatalog modes remain compatible. Saved artifacts keep their original meaning.

This is a bounded migration consumer of the new character layer, not reversed
"building-first" land planning. The existing World is intentionally unchanged.
Demand admission, varied horizontal parcels, distinct five downtown master
planners and final campus/skyline acceptance remain separate unfinished work.
